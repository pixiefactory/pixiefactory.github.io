Wombats Poop CUBES?!

The fact

Wombats poop cubes! 🟫 Bare-nosed wombats from Australia make cube-shaped poop, about 80 to 100 pieces a night. Scientists found the cubes form in the last part of the wombat's intestine, where stiffer and stretchier sections of the gut wall squeeze the poop into flat sides and sharp corners. Wombats leave the cubes on rocks and logs to mark their turf, and cubes don't roll away! The research won the 2019 Ig Nobel Prize in Physics.
