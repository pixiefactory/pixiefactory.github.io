Bananas Are Berries… Strawberries Aren't?!

Your strawberry isn't a berry... but your banana is?!

The fact

Botanically, bananas are true berries — but strawberries aren't! 🍌🍓 A berry grows from one flower with a single ovary (soft flesh, usually lots of seeds). Bananas check every box, and so do grapes, tomatoes, and oranges. A strawberry is an accessory fruit: the juicy red part is the flower's swollen base, and those little yellow “seeds” on the outside are the real fruits (achenes).

What Pixie says

In botany, a berry grows from one flower with a single ovary — soft flesh, and usually lots of seeds.

Bananas check every box. So do grapes, tomatoes, and oranges!

A strawberry? The juicy red part isn't even the fruit — it's the flower's swollen base.

Those little yellow dots on the outside? Those are the real fruits, called achenes!

So bananas are berries... and strawberries are the rebels of the fruit bowl!
