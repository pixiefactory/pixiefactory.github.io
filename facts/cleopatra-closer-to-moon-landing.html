The fact

Cleopatra lived closer in time to the Apollo 11 Moon landing than to the building of the Great Pyramid of Giza! 🏛️🚀 The Great Pyramid was built for the pharaoh Khufu around 4,500 years ago. By the time Cleopatra was born (around 69 BC), it was already more than 2,000 years old, and the Moon landing in 1969 came about 2,000 years after her. When it was new, the pyramid was covered in smooth white limestone and stood about 147 meters tall.
